# Lyndon Signature Plus — placeholder image prompts

Placeholders for showing the website only. **Replace them all with real photos before launch.**

## How to use

1. Paste the **prompt**, then add the **style suffix** to the end of it.
2. Use the **negative prompt** if your tool supports one (Midjourney: `--no …`, SD/Flux: negative field).
3. Use the aspect ratio given. Export **at least 1600px wide** (the hero at 2000px+).
4. Save with the **file name** shown, into `website/assets/img/`. Files marked *(existing)* replace a current image and appear on the site straight away. Files marked *(new slot)* need me to add them to the page.

### Style suffix (add to every prompt)

> Realistic photograph, taken in Nigeria, shot on a full-frame camera with a 24mm lens at chest height, straight-on composition, natural daylight mixed with warm interior lights, true-to-life colours, subtle real-world imperfections (a visible wall socket, uneven light falloff, slight scuffs), editorial architectural photography, not a 3D render.

### Negative prompt

> 3D render, CGI, video game, oversaturated, HDR glow, lens flare, too many potted plants, perfectly symmetrical staging, fisheye, tilted horizon, watermark, garbled text, misspelled letters, extra logos, people posing at camera, cartoon, illustration

### Rules so they don't look "AI"

- **Logo on 2 images only** (#2 and #3). Every other sign uses a plain made-up name or no text. Seeing the Lyndon logo in every photo was the biggest giveaway on the flyers.
- **Text gets garbled.** For #2 and #3, either use a tool that handles text well (Ideogram, GPT-Image, Nano Banana) or generate a blank sign and drop the real logo on in Canva or Photoshop.
- **Make it Nigerian.** Lagos/Anambra architecture, local light, generator box or AC units outside, Nigerian people where people appear.
- **Mix the moods.** Don't make every room warm-gold. Some daylight-white, some evening.
- **Generate 3–4 options per prompt** and pick the least "perfect" one.
